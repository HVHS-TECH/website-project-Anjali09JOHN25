Kangaroo

Kangaroo is one of the most iconic animals in Australia and is commonly found in bushland, grasslands, and open areas around the country, including parts of Queensland. They are known for their powerful hind legs, strong tails, and ability to hop at high speeds across long distances. Kangaroos usually live in groups called mobs and are most active during the early morning and evening when it is cooler. They feed mainly on grasses and plants and are well adapted to Australia’s dry and varied environments.

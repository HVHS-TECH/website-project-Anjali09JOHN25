Some history about the Gold Coast:

The Gold Coast has a history that begins with its Traditional Custodians, the Yugambeh-speaking Aboriginal peoples. For thousands of years, they lived along the coast and hinterland, relying on the land and sea for food, while also holding strong cultural and spiritual connections to the area. European settlement began in the 1800s, mainly focused on logging valuable timber and small-scale farming. At this stage, the region was still quiet and mostly undeveloped. In the early 1900s, tourism started to grow as people were attracted to the warm weather and beaches. Better transport links made it easier for visitors to travel there, especially from nearby cities. By the mid-20th century, the name “Gold Coast” became widely used, and the area rapidly developed into a tourist destination with hotels, entertainment, and later high-rise buildings. Today, the Gold Coast is one of Australia’s most popular coastal cities, known for surfing, theme parks, and tourism, while still reflecting its natural and cultural origins.
